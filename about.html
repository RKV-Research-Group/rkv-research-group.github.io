Biography

Dr Varma's work sits at the meeting point of experimental testing and numerical simulation of concrete and composite structures.

He received his B.Tech in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati and his PhD in Structural Engineering from the University of Minho, Portugal. His doctoral research developed numerical models for simulating the cyclic behaviour of reinforced concrete structures incorporating new advanced materials. At Minho he carried out large-scale tests on reinforced concrete columns and implemented a 3D Timoshenko fibre model in the displacement-based finite element software FEMIX.

He joined IIT Jammu in 2017 as Assistant Professor and was promoted to Associate Professor in 2024. He served as Head of the Department of Civil Engineering from 2018 to 2020.

His expertise spans fibre-reinforced concrete, constitutive model development and its implementation in finite element programs, and strengthening of RC members with carbon-fibre-reinforced polymers. At IIT Jammu he has broadened this foundation into architected metamaterials, wave propagation in micropolar composite panels, wind engineering for hilly terrain and precast prestressed concrete pavements. He also advises public agencies and industry on the design, proof checking and testing of bridges and buildings across Jammu & Kashmir and India.
