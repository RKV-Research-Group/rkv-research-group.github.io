Biography

Dr Varma's work sits at the meeting point of experimental testing and numerical simulation of concrete and composite structures.

He received his B.Tech in Civil Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati, including a research stay at the University of L'Aquila, Italy, working on the vibration analysis of cable-stayed bridges. He then spent seven years at the University of Minho, Portugal — first as a Research Engineer and Research Associate, then as an FCT-funded doctoral researcher — where he carried out large-scale tests on RC columns and developed hysteretic material models and nonlinear fibre-element, shell and plate formulations in the finite element software FEMIX.

Between 2012 and 2017 he worked in industry on major international infrastructure: as Bridge and Tunnel Engineer at COWI on the Køge North–Næstved bridges in Denmark, underground stations of the Doha Metro in Qatar and the 12.4 km Puente Nigale bridge over Lake Maracaibo in Venezuela; as Structural Engineer at Bechtel Civil Infrastructure; and as Design Manager at Aarvee Associates, Hyderabad.

He joined IIT Jammu in December 2017 as Assistant Professor and became Associate Professor in July 2024. He served as Head of the Department of Civil Engineering from 2018 to 2020.

His expertise spans fibre-reinforced concrete, constitutive model development and its implementation in finite element programs, and strengthening of RC members with carbon-fibre-reinforced polymers. At IIT Jammu he has broadened this foundation into architected metamaterials, wave propagation in micropolar composite panels, wind engineering for hilly terrain and precast prestressed concrete pavements. He also advises public agencies and industry on the design, proof checking and testing of bridges and buildings across Jammu & Kashmir and India.

Industry & research

Selected projects before IIT Jammu

Five years of design practice with international engineering firms, and seven years of applied research in Portugal.

COWI · Denmark

Køge North–Næstved bridges

Design of ten solid-slab road bridges (≈10 m spans, 9–36° skew) with portal-frame and integral-abutment arrangement, to Eurocodes. Client: Banedanmark.

COWI · Qatar

Doha Metro — Al Bidda & Al Wahda

Design of underground station structures and switch boxes — lining, end and intermediate walls, base slabs, ventilation and emergency shafts — to Eurocodes. Client: Qatar Rail.

COWI · Venezuela

Puente Nigale bridge, Lake Maracaibo

Basic and detailed design for the 12.4 km road-and-rail bridge: sub- and superstructure analysis of the West Short Span Bridge and substructure of the East Elevated Bridge, including 1.8 m composite piles. Client: Odebrecht.

University of Minho · Portugal

SFRSCC lightweight façade panels

Production system for self-compacting steel-fibre-reinforced concrete panels, durability characterisation and the cross-section analysis software DOCROS.

University of Minho · Portugal

Seismic assessment & FRP strengthening

Pushover analysis of a deficiently designed nine-storey building in Bragança; cyclic tests of FRP-strengthened columns monitored with optical-fibre sensors.

University of L'Aquila · Italy

Cable-stayed bridges

Vibration analysis of cable-stayed bridges during an undergraduate research stay.
