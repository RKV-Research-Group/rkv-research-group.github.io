Overview

Dr Varma brings research-grade analysis and testing to practical engineering problems.

His professional work covers structural design and assessment, bridge engineering and proof checking, finite element modelling, experimental investigation and engineering problem-solving. It has involved government organisations, public-sector agencies and industry partners on a range of infrastructure projects, with particular experience of the terrain, climate and seismic demands of Jammu & Kashmir.

This builds on five years of design practice before joining IIT Jammu — with COWI on the Køge North–Næstved bridges in Denmark, the Doha Metro in Qatar and the Puente Nigale bridge in Venezuela; with Bechtel Civil Infrastructure; and as Design Manager at Aarvee Associates. Selected projects →
