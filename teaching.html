Teaching

Undergraduate and postgraduate courses in mechanics, structural analysis and concrete design at IIT Jammu.

Courses

Courses taught

A sequence that takes students from the fundamentals of mechanics to the analysis and design of real structures.

Core

Engineering Mechanics

Foundations of statics and dynamics for engineering students: forces and equilibrium, the analysis of trusses and frames, friction, centroids and moments of inertia, and the kinematics and kinetics of particles and rigid bodies.

Core

Mechanics of Solids

How solid bodies deform and carry load: stress and strain, axial, bending and torsional members, shear force and bending moment, deflection of beams, combined stresses and the buckling of columns.

Structures

Structural Analysis

Analysis of determinate and indeterminate beams, frames and trusses using classical and matrix methods, influence lines and energy methods — the basis for modern structural software.

Design

Design of RCC Structures

Limit-state design of reinforced concrete members to Indian Standards: beams, slabs, columns and footings, with attention to detailing, serviceability and practical design.

Supervision

Research supervision is an extension of teaching.

Dr Varma has supervised three PhD theses to completion, with three doctoral scholars currently in the group, alongside M.Tech and undergraduate research projects. Former students now hold positions at the University of Southampton, the Model Institute of Engineering and Technology, Jammu, and IIT Jammu.
